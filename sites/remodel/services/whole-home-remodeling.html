Make the house work as a whole

A whole-home remodel starts with the way you use the space. Tell us which rooms need to change, what should remain and whether the project includes structural changes, an addition or a new layout. The first goal is a clear brief that can become a comparable scope and estimate.

Start with priorities and a complete budget

Separate must-have work from optional upgrades. Budget for design and approvals where needed, demolition, structural work, plumbing and electrical, materials, installation, finishes and contingency. Product allowances should state what they buy and how differences will be handled.

Decide whether to remodel in phases

Working room by room can help preserve some daily routines, but it can also affect sequencing and repeat work. Discuss temporary kitchen or bathroom access, protection for occupied rooms, storage and whether you will need to stay elsewhere during a construction phase.

Set responsibilities before construction

Ask who prepares the drawings, submits for permits, purchases materials, schedules trades and manages inspections. Confirm the licensed contractor responsible for construction in your written agreement. A planning conversation or website estimate is not a construction contract.

Compare the same scope

Use the same room list, drawings and finish schedule when reviewing estimates. Mark exclusions and unresolved allowances. Discuss concealed conditions, owner changes and the process for approving additional work before the project begins.

Tell The Home Remodel Group what you want to change

Share your ZIP code, photos, current layout if available, priority rooms, budget and desired timing. We will discuss the project and the next step for an estimate. For panelized ADU or addition materials with your own contractor, HRG Prefab is our specialized starting point.
