About HRG

Years of remodels, written down

The Home Remodel Group remodels homes: kitchens, bathrooms, room additions, ADUs, garage conversions and whole houses. We've been doing this for years, and this site is where we share what that work has taught us.

Who we are

The Home Remodel Group does the projects that make a house work better for the people living in it, from a single bathroom to additions, ADUs and full renovations. HRG Prefab, our prefab division, builds ADUs and additions from prefabricated materials made off-site.

Many American homes are decades old, with plaster walls, original wiring, galvanized plumbing and quirks nobody wrote down. That's why we care so much about planning. The surprises in an older house are easier to handle when you expect them.

Why we wrote these guides

Homeowners ask us the same good questions on almost every job. What will this really cost? Do I need a permit? How long will we be without a kitchen? How do I know a contractor is legitimate? The answers shouldn't depend on who you happen to call first, so we put them here for anyone planning a project, whether or not you hire us.

A written scope, every time

Every price we give is built on a written scope that says what is and isn't included, so you can compare it with anyone else's.

Honest timelines

We quote realistic schedules, including permits and material lead times, and tell you early if something on site changes the plan.

Permits and inspections handled

We plan the permit path at the start and schedule inspections as the work moves, so the job closes out properly.

One point of contact

From the first visit to the final walkthrough, you know who to call.
