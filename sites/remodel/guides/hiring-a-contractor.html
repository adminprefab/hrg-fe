Five checks that protect you on any project.

What to know

  • Look up the license and complaints on your state licensing board's website.
  • Ask for insurance certificates sent by the insurer: liability and workers' comp.
  • Compare bids on the same written scope.
  • Pay as work is done, never far ahead. Some states cap deposits; California allows $1,000 or 10%, whichever is less.
  • Put changes in writing before extra work starts, and collect lien waivers when you pay.
