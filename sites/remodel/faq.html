Remodeling FAQs

Plan with clear answers

How do I start a remodeling estimate?

Tell us your project type, ZIP code and contact details using the estimate form. The team follows up to review your goals and the property before confirming a written scope.

Is the online cost planner a final quote?

No. The cost planner provides a planning estimate. A final scope and price depend on the property, dimensions, finishes, site conditions, permits and contractor review.

What should I include in a remodeling budget?

Account for design, permits, materials, labor, demolition, site work, utility changes and a contingency. Ask for a written scope showing what is included and which items are allowances or exclusions.

Do kitchen and bathroom remodels require permits?

Permit requirements depend on your location and scope. Structural, electrical, plumbing and mechanical changes may need local review. Confirm requirements with the local building department before work begins.

Can you help with an ADU or home addition?

Yes. Start with a property and scope review. For panelized prefab ADUs and building materials, explore HRG Prefab. Lot conditions, utilities, local approvals and the agreed contractor scope determine what is feasible.

Who performs the construction?

Confirm the licensed general contractor and each party's responsibilities in your written project agreement. HRG Prefab coordinates planning and materials for its prefab scopes; the homeowner hires the general contractor for site construction and assembly.
