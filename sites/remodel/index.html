How it works

Five steps from idea to keys back in your hand

  1. Plan and budgetDecide what has to change and get a price range for your area.
  2. DesignDrawings when walls, plumbing or the footprint change, plus your finish choices.
  3. PermitsOver-the-counter for simple work, plan check for additions and structural changes.
  4. Build and inspectDemolition, rough-in, inspections, then drywall and finishes.
  5. Final walkthroughFinal inspection, punch list, lien releases and warranties.

Questions

Before you call us

Where do you work?

Across Los Angeles County. The cost planner also adjusts prices for other areas, so you can compare.

How accurate is the cost planner?

It gives planning ranges built from Los Angeles County prices for materials, labor and sales tax, adjusted for your area. Real prices depend on site conditions, access, hidden damage and the exact products you choose, so HRG confirms every number after a site visit.

How do I get a firm quote?

Build your estimate in the planner and send it to HRG from there, or call 213.216.4416 or email sales@hrgprefab.com. We'll set up a site visit and give you a written scope and price.

What is HRG Prefab?

HRG Prefab is a division of The Home Remodel Group. It builds ADUs and additions from prefabricated materials made off-site, then assembles them on your property. Visit hrgprefab.com to see the models.
