Before you call

Helpful to have ready

The address

It tells us which building department reviews the work and whether any local rules, like hillside, flood, wildfire or historic district rules, apply.

Photos and rough sizes

A few photos of the space and approximate measurements help us prepare for the visit.

The year the house was built

Homes built before 1978 need lead and asbestos testing before demolition, which affects the plan.
